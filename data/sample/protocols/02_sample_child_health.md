# Protocol: Child Health and Immunisation (Sample Only)

> NOTICE: This is a placeholder demonstration document provided for testing search indexing. It does not constitute official clinical advice.

## IMM-1.1: Primary Vaccination Schedule
Newborns receive BCG, OPV-0, and Hepatitis B at birth. Pentavalent-1 and Rota-1 are administered at 6 weeks of age.

## CHD-1.1: Management of Diarrhoea and Dehydration
Administer Oral Rehydration Salts (ORS) and Zinc dispersible tablets (20 mg daily for 14 days) to prevent severe dehydration during acute watery diarrhoea.

## NUT-1.1: Malnutrition Screening via MUAC
Mid-Upper Arm Circumference (MUAC) color bands indicate nutritional status: Red (< 11.5 cm) indicates Severe Acute Malnutrition (SAM), Yellow (11.5 - 12.5 cm) indicates Moderate Acute Malnutrition (MAM), and Green (> 12.5 cm) indicates Normal nutrition.
