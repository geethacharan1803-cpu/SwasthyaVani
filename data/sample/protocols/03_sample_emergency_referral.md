# Protocol: Emergency Referral and Red Flags (Sample Only)

> NOTICE: This is a placeholder demonstration document provided for testing search indexing. It does not constitute official clinical advice.

## ESC-1.1: Severe Obstetric Danger Signs
Immediate ambulance transfer (108/102) is required for heavy vaginal bleeding, convulsions or seizures, severe persistent headache with blurred vision, and high fever during pregnancy or labour.

## ESC-2.1: Neonatal Red Flags
Transfer urgently if the infant is cold to touch despite skin-to-skin contact, has chest indrawing or grunting, is unable to feed, or presents with yellow soles and palms (severe jaundice).

## SCP-1.1: Scope Boundaries and Medical Officer Referral
Frontline workers do not prescribe antibiotics or injectables. If high-grade fever persists beyond 48 hours or suspected bacterial infection occurs, immediately refer to the Medical Officer at the Primary Health Centre.
