# Protocol: Maternal Health and Antenatal Care (Sample Only)

> NOTICE: This is a placeholder demonstration document provided for testing search indexing. It does not constitute official clinical advice.

## ANC-1.1: Antenatal Registration and Scheduling
Every pregnant woman should be registered within the first 12 weeks of pregnancy. Four standard antenatal visits are scheduled to monitor maternal health, fetal growth, and blood pressure.

## ANC-1.2: Nutritional Care and Iron Folic Acid
Administer 180 tablets of Iron Folic Acid (IFA) starting from the second trimester to prevent maternal anaemia. Advise a balanced diet with green leafy vegetables and dairy.

## ANC-1.3: Identification of High Risk Pregnancy
Identify high-risk factors including severe anaemia (Hb < 7 g/dL), pre-eclampsia (BP >= 140/90 with proteinuria), previous Caesarean section, and maternal age under 18 or over 35.
